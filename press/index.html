One career record, built from photos

Ocean Link has passed 1,000 downloads on Google Play. The app keeps a seafarer's certificates, sea time, CV and job applications in one place, alongside lessons, maritime news and a network of other seafarers.

Its founder, Marko Peruško, is a serving 2nd Officer on LNG tankers. He built the platform while at sea, to fix two problems he had lived through himself: retyping the same career details for every agency, and certificates that expire without warning.

A seafarer uploads a photo or PDF of a certificate and the app reads the certificate name, number, issue and expiry dates and issuing authority. Sea service is read the same way from discharge book pages. The seafarer checks every field before saving, and the CV is generated from those records.

“As a fresh 3rd Officer I typed the same details into about 50 agency websites by hand. Later, certificates expired on board without me noticing until just before joining. Ocean Link is the tool I needed then.”

Marko Peruško 2nd Officer and founder, Ocean Link

What is in the app

Every feature, screen by screen

Eight parts of one app. Each works on its own, and each feeds the others: a certificate added once shows up in the CV, the job application and the agency's view.

Certificates

Every certificate, with the time it has left

One list for STCW certificates, flag state endorsements, medicals and travel documents, sorted by what needs action first.

  • Add by photo or PDF. The app reads the certificate name, number, issue and expiry dates and issuing authority.
  • The seafarer stays in control. Every field is checked before it is saved.
  • Reminders at 90, 60 and 30 days before a certificate expires.
  • Travel documents in their own group: passport, discharge book and vaccination record.
Sea time

Sea service read straight from the discharge book

No spreadsheet and no counting days by hand. The record builds itself contract by contract.

  • A photo of a discharge book page gives the vessel, rank and sign-on and sign-off dates.
  • Grouped by rank, with the time served in each rank added up.
  • Total sea time and last sign-off always at the top.
  • A free sea time calculator is open to anyone at oceanlinkai.com.
Sea Card

A career at sea as one number

Sea Card turns the sea time record into something a seafarer wants to show people.

  • Every day at sea counted since the first contract.
  • Time at sea against time at home, shown as a simple split.
  • Made to share with shipmates, family and friends.
Digital CV

A professional CV, free

Every seafarer gets this CV at no cost. It builds itself from the certificates and sea service already in the app, so it is never out of date.

  • No payment. Ocean Link is free for seafarers, and the CV is part of it.
  • Everything a crewing manager checks: sea service with totals by vessel type, then every travel document and certificate with its status.
  • Shared as a live link with any agency, or saved as a PDF.
  • Upload an old CV and the app reads it into the profile.
  • Free CV examples for 12 ranks, from Master to Ordinary Seaman.
Jobs

Vacancies by rank, applied for with one button

The job board knows the seafarer's rank, so the right vacancies come first.

  • Filter by rank and vessel type. A Matches tab shows the jobs for the seafarer's own rank.
  • One-button applications that go from the seafarer's own email straight to the agency.
  • Email alerts for new vacancies in the seafarer's rank.
  • Saved and applied jobs tracked in their own tabs.
Agency directory

Check the licence before signing

A directory of crewing agencies taken from official government registers.

  • Nine registers: India, the Philippines, Ukraine, Bulgaria, Romania, Croatia, Latvia, Lithuania and Georgia.
  • Each agency page shows its licence details and the register they come from.
  • Agencies claim their page for free and post vacancies on it.
Learn

Short lessons, each with a quiz

Video lessons made for a watch break, not a classroom.

  • COLREG rules: lights, shapes, sound signals and steering and sailing rules.
  • Lessons from real incidents, from Ever Given to Exxon Valdez.
  • Onboard safety and ship systems: mooring, pilot ladders, lifeboats, steering gear, main engine.
  • A quiz after every lesson.
SeaFeed, crew and chat

The professional network for seafarers

A feed built around life and work at sea, with the people a seafarer has actually sailed with.

  • One feed, five tabs: all posts, learning, news, resources and crew.
  • “I'm available” posts show a seafarer is ready to join, with CV, sea time and certificates one tap away.
  • Find shipmates from past vessels and message them directly.
  • Maritime news in the same place.

Screens show a demo profile. Agency names in the job list are hidden.

For crewing agencies

The CVs in the inbox become a candidate pool

Recruitment desk is the agency side of Ocean Link. It answers the question a crewing manager asks first: who can join on this date and stay valid until sign-off?

Recruitment desk in Ocean Link. Sample candidates shown.

  • Import CVs in bulk. Each one becomes a candidate record with rank, sea time and certificates.
  • Set the joining date and contract length. The desk shows who stays valid to sign-off and who needs a renewal first.
  • Filter by rank, vessel type, time in rank and availability.
  • Shortlist candidates and post vacancies from the same screen.
  • Private by design. Imported candidates are visible only to that agency.
  • Free to try on 20 of the agency's own CVs.

Questions and answers

What journalists ask first

How is it different from job boards and crew software?

The records belong to the seafarer and keep themselves current, so agencies receive up-to-date data as a by-product. Ocean Link uses AI only to read documents. It does not rank or filter candidates.

Who sees a seafarer's data?

A profile is visible to crewing agencies only when the seafarer allows it in Settings, and Ocean Link does not sell personal data. Documents are uploaded only after the seafarer gives consent.

From medical certificates the app keeps only the name, number and dates. A seafarer can delete the account, with its certificates and sea time, from Settings at any time.

What does it mean for crewing agencies?

Agencies can claim their page and post vacancies at no cost, and applications arrive directly from seafarers whose certificates and sea time are current. Recruitment desk turns the CVs an agency already receives by email into a searchable candidate list.

What comes next?

An iOS app is being prepared for the App Store. More lessons are being added to the Learn section.
