# Ocean Link social content plan

Two posts a day, every day, scheduled in Metricool (brand id 7716383, timezone Asia/Tomsk).
Approach: **entertain first, then educate, then sell** — the app is the payoff, never the opener.

## Strategy: macro out (from 30 Oct 2026)

Model: Jefferson Fisher, a Texas trial lawyer with about 6M Instagram followers. He grew by posting about everyday communication, which everyone deals with, not about law, which nobody follows. His clients are still inside that wider audience. Ocean Link does the same:

- **At least 4 of every 5 posts are about surviving a seafarer career and life at sea** (crewing agents, contracts, CVs, interviews, onboard conflict, rest hours, sign-off, first contract, family at home), the things every seafarer and their family cares about. Ocean Link is the soft payoff in the CTA slide or caption line, never the topic.
- **At most 1 in 5 posts comes from the Ocean Link or Founder pillars.**
- **"Survival guide · NN" is the running series** for macro-out carousels. Continue the numbering from the last one in the Log (week 1 used 01–06).
- Structure: slide 1 hook; 3–4 points with **the strongest point last** (it trains people to swipe to the end); CTA asks about real experience.
- Hook formula that works: "How to know if [someone in your life] is [doing X to you]", e.g. "How to know a crewing agent is scamming you". Rotate it with the other hook styles in PLAYBOOK §1.

## Pillars (rotate; never two from the same pillar on one day)

- **Sea life** (entertain): relatable humour, watch life, sign-off, ship Wi-Fi, mess food, shipmates. No faces, no ship or company names.
- **Know-how** (educate): COLREGs, STCW/certificate validity, sign-off paperwork, CV and interview tips for crewing agencies.
- **Ocean Link** (sell, soft): Sea Card, certificate tracker, CV in one tap, sea time tracker, Learn tab COLREG quizzes, licensed agencies (RPSL) shown in the app.
- **Founder** (trust): built at sea by a serving 2nd Officer.
- **Community** (engage): questions and polls that get seafarers commenting.

## Hard rules

- Follow social/PLAYBOOK.md for hooks, captions, links and hashtags on every network.
- A **draft** post in Metricool counts as a filled slot. Never create a second post for a network/slot that already has a draft (Marko approves drafts himself).

- Every regulation/certificate fact is checked against an official or reputable source before posting (IMO/COLREG text, STCW, MLC, flag-state pages). If it can't be confirmed, drop that line or pick another topic. Rules can differ by flag state — say so where it matters.
- Never post job vacancies or anything that looks like advertising another agency's vacancy (legal demand from Danica, Sept 2026).
- No invented numbers: no user counts, success rates, salaries or testimonials. No made-up quotes from real people.
- No AI-generated faces or people; no real people's faces; no ship names, company names or logos other than Ocean Link.
- Wording: rank is "2nd Officer"; certificates are **snapped**, not "scanned"; never call the app "beta"; app is Android-only (Google Play) — never mention iOS/App Store.
- Founder facts that are true and usable: started as a fresh 3rd Officer with no contract in rank, typing the same details into ~50 agency websites with a weak CV; later saw certificates quietly expire onboard until a last-minute scramble before joining. Nothing beyond that.
- Design: only the `social/slide.html` template (abyss-dark brand). Valid status = cyan #00B0D2, warning #F5A524, critical #F2555A.
