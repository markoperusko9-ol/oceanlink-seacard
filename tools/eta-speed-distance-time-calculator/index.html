ETA, speed, distance and time calculator

Work out an ETA across time zones, the speed you need to make a pilot station on time, or any one of speed, distance and time from the other two.

Time (h, min)

Fill in two. The tool works out the one you leave empty.

How it works

Time = Distance ÷ Speed  ·  Speed = Distance ÷ Time  ·  Distance = Speed × Time

For an ETA the tool first turns your departure time into UTC, adds the steaming time, and then turns the result into the clock kept at the arrival port. That is the safe order: do the sums in UTC and convert to ship's time only at the end.

Choose the clock by its offset from UTC. East of Greenwich is plus, west is minus. If the ship or the port keeps daylight saving time, pick the offset actually in use.

Worked example

You leave at 08:00 on the 23rd, keeping UTC+1, with 1,200 nm to go at 12 kn. Steaming time = 1,200 ÷ 12 = 100 hours, which is 4 days 4 hours. Departure is 07:00 UTC on the 23rd, so the ETA is 11:00 UTC on the 27th. The arrival port keeps UTC+8, so the ETA in local time is 19:00 on the 27th.

How this tool was checked

The time arithmetic is tested on fixed cases, including a passage across the date line from UTC+12 to UTC−11, where the local arrival time comes out on the same calendar day you left.

Limits

  • The speed is the average you will make good over the ground. Allow for current, weather and slow steaming in pilotage yourself.
  • The tool does not know when clocks will be advanced or retarded on passage. It only needs the clock at each end.

FAQ

How do I calculate a ship's ETA across time zones?

Convert the departure time to UTC, add the steaming time (distance divided by speed), then convert the result to the time kept at the arrival port. Doing the sums in UTC avoids mistakes with clock changes on passage.

What speed do I need to arrive at a set time?

Find the time available in UTC between departure and the required arrival, then divide the distance by that time. The Required speed tab does this, including the time zones at both ends.
