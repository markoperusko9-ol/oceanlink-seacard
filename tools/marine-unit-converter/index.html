The Beaufort force is read from the WMO table using the speed in whole knots. Results are shown to six significant figures.

Volume to mass multiplies cubic metres by the density in tonnes per cubic metre. That suits ballast, fresh water and quick estimates. It is not a cargo calculation: oil, gas and chemical quantities need the temperature and density corrections from the tables named in the charter party.

How this tool was checked

Every group is tested against published conversion factors, for example 1 bar = 14.5038 psi, 1 m³ = 6.28981 US barrels and 100 °C = 212 °F, and against every boundary of the Beaufort table.

FAQ

How many metres per second is one knot?

One knot is 1,852 metres per hour, which is 0.514444 metres per second. To go the other way, 1 m/s is 1.94384 knots.

How long is a shackle of anchor cable?

A shackle is 27.5 metres in modern metric cable. The traditional length is 15 fathoms, which is 27.43 metres.
