Shore jobs after sailing: options for officers and engineers

Quick answer

Senior deck and engine officers can move ashore as government surveyor, class or marine surveyor, superintendent, DPA/HSQE, crewing staff or training institute faculty, and most good roles ask for a management-level CoC plus command or chief/second engineer time. In India, DG Shipping surveyor posts are filled through UPSC advertisements, and teaching posts in approved institutes follow STCW Section A-I/6 and DG Shipping's training rules. Plan the move two or three contracts ahead, and keep your CoC revalidated so you can go back to sea if you need to.

The realistic map of shore roles

No single ladder takes you ashore. Each employer sets its own entry bar. In practice the bar depends on three things: the CoC you hold, time served in senior rank and what you have done beyond watchkeeping, such as dry docks, vetting, audits and cargo claims.

Role Usual background Who sets the requirement
Government nautical / engineer surveyor (India: DG Shipping) Master FG or MEO Class I with senior sea time Recruitment rules, published through UPSC adverts

Marine / cargo / P&I / bunker surveyor Master, Chief Officer or Chief Engineer The firm, and sometimes the port or insurer

DPA, HSQE, internal auditor Senior officer with ISM audit training The company, under the ISM Code

Pilot, harbour master, loading / mooring master Usually Master with command time The port authority or terminal

Where the requirement comes from a company or port and not from a convention, it varies. Read each advert carefully. Do not assume one employer's rules apply to the next.

Government surveyor posts in India (DG Shipping via UPSC)

The Directorate General of Shipping recruits its technical surveyors directly through the Union Public Service Commission. You apply online against a specific advertisement, not to DG Shipping. The essential qualifications in UPSC adverts published between 2017 and 2022 were:

  • Nautical Surveyor-cum-Deputy Director General (Technical): a CoC as Master of a foreign-going ship, plus 5 years as a deck officer after obtaining your Second Mate (FG) CoC. At least one of those years must be in command of a foreign-going ship. Extra Master or an MSc in Maritime Affairs has been listed as desirable, and so has survey or examination experience.
  • Engineer and Ship Surveyor-cum-Deputy Director General (Technical): a CoC as MEO Class I (or a recognised equivalent), plus five years at sea, of which one year is as Chief Engineer or Second Engineer. The recruitment rules for the engineering cadre were revised in 2025. Always check the essential qualifications in the current UPSC advertisement.
  • Ship Surveyor (naval architecture side): a naval architecture degree plus years of design, construction, survey or repair experience. This post is not normally open to seagoing engineers on CoC alone.

Process in outline: 1. Watch the UPSC recruitment advertisements and DG Shipping notices. 2. Check the age limit, the closing date and any category relaxations in that advert. These change from one advert to the next. 3. Apply online and upload your CoC, discharge book pages and service testimonials, ideally in the format the advert asks for. 4. When UPSC receives many applications, it may shortlist using criteria above the minimum (see the advert's notes). It also rejects applications when the experience certificates attached do not support the experience claimed. 5. Interview.

Common rejections: - no certificate showing command or CE/2E time - sea service that does not add up because sign-on and sign-off dates are missing - experience certificates that do not support the experience claimed (UPSC rejects these as LEQ-B) - applying with a CoC that is not the grade the advert asks for

Filipino officers: MARINA posts are government positions filled under civil service rules. Check the Notices of Filling Up of Vacant Positions on the official MARINA website (marina.gov.ph) and the STCW Office careers page (stcw.marina.gov.ph/careers). Do not rely on third-party job boards.

Teaching and assessing: faculty at approved training institutes

Training institutes in India are a steady entry point, especially for officers who want to stay connected to seafarers. Your qualifications come from two layers of rules.

  • STCW Regulation I/6 and Section A-I/6. Anyone who trains or assesses seafarers for certification must be qualified for the type and level of training or assessment involved. Section A-I/8 brings instructor qualifications into the institute's quality standards.
  • DG Shipping's training rules for approved MTIs. The Training Manual requires principals, faculty and instructors to meet A-I/6. Minimum CoC levels are set per course in the course guidelines. Principals, faculty and instructors must complete the DG Shipping-approved Vertical Integration Course for Trainers (VICT), which is based on IMO Model Course 6.09, Training Course for Instructors. Faculty who completed TOTA/TOTI before 1 October 2018 are exempt.

In practice: 1. Senior faculty posts often ask for Chief Mate FG / MEO Class II or higher, and some institutes prefer Master FG / MEO Class I. The minimum is set in each DG Shipping course guideline. 2. Rating and petty officer backgrounds can lead to instructor posts on practical and basic courses. 3. Simulator instructors may also need simulator-specific instructor training (IMO Model Course 6.10) and type training on the equipment.

Course-level minimum qualifications are revised from time to time. Before you apply, check the current DG Shipping course guidelines for the course you want to teach.

Ship management, class and the private surveying side

Superintendents. Technical superintendents are usually former Chief or Second Engineers. Marine superintendents are usually Masters or Chief Officers. The interview tests budgets, dry-dock specifications, PMS backlogs, defect closure, PSC detention follow-up and how you handle a Master or Chief Engineer under pressure. Dry-dock experience as a senior engineer often decides who gets the job.

DPA / HSQE. The ISM Code requires the company to designate a person with direct access to the highest level of management. The Code leaves the person's qualifications to the company, and guidance expects audit training and relevant experience. A recognised ISM/ISO internal auditor or lead auditor course helps.

Vetting inspectors and auditors. Oil major vetting schemes accredit their own inspectors. The criteria are set by the scheme operator, not by any administration. Check the operator's current criteria before spending money on courses.

Classification societies. Each society recruits and trains its own surveyors. Expect structured internal training and a probation period before you sign anything on your own.

Independent marine surveyors. Typical work is cargo, condition, on/off-hire, bunker quantity and P&I surveys. Cargo and tanker experience counts for a lot here. Some ports and terminals require local registration or licensing, which differs by country.

Worked scenarios: planning the move

Scenario 1: Second Engineer, MEO Class II, aiming for technical superintendent. 1. Pass MEO Class I and get Chief Engineer sea time first. Many managers advertise for CE experience. 2. On the next contracts, ask to lead dry-dock jobs. Keep your own record of the specifications you wrote and the jobs you closed. 3. Build a short CV section on major overhauls, the PSC and class findings you closed, and PMS work. 4. Ask your own manager about an office attachment between contracts. Many people get their first shore job this way.

Scenario 2: Master, 18 months in command, aiming for DG Shipping Nautical Surveyor. 1. Count your deck officer service after your Second Mate FG CoC and check it against the current advert. Remember that UPSC may shortlist above the minimum when it receives many applications. 2. Get the Master's service certificate and testimonials from each company, on letterhead and signed. Make sure they clearly support the experience you claim. 3. Consider the desirable qualifications (Extra Master or an MSc in Maritime Affairs) if you have the time. 4. Keep your CoC and medical current while you wait. Recruitment cycles are slow.

Scenario 3: Chief Officer who wants to teach. Apply to an approved MTI for courses where your CoC meets the course guideline minimum. Complete VICT early, unless you are exempt through TOTA/TOTI done before 1 October 2018. Experience as a shipboard assessor or TAR supervisor helps at interview.

Keep your CoC alive ashore

If you leave sea service, your CoC does not revalidate itself. Under STCW Regulation I/11 and Section A-I/11, revalidation at intervals of no more than five years needs proof of medical fitness and continued professional competence. Competence can be shown in any of these ways:

  • approved seagoing service of at least 12 months in total during the preceding five years
  • performing functions the administration considers equivalent to sea service
  • passing an approved test, or completing an approved course
  • completing approved seagoing service of at least 3 months as a supernumerary, or in a lower rank, immediately before taking up the rank the certificate is for

National rules may set out these routes in more detail. Which shore jobs count as equivalent service is decided by each administration, not by STCW. Do not assume your superintendent or faculty years will count. Check with DG Shipping (India), MARINA (Philippines) or your flag administration before your CoC expires.

Also keep your STCW refresher courses up to date. If you let everything lapse, going back to sea becomes expensive and slow.

Oral-board style: questions shore interviewers actually ask

Shore interviews feel a lot like an MMD oral. Expect questions like these:

  • A vessel is detained by PSC for a fire damper defect. Walk me through the next 48 hours. They want root cause, a corrective action plan, informing flag and class, and a fleet-wide check.
  • How does the DPA differ from the Master's overriding authority? They want ISM Code clauses 4 and 5 explained in your own words.
  • What qualifies you to assess a candidate rather than just train one? They want STCW A-I/6 and the VICT / Model Course 6.09 basis.
  • Your CoC expires in eight months and you are ashore. What are your options? They want the A-I/11 routes and to hear that the administration decides what counts as equivalent service.

Common reasons candidates are rejected:

  • a CV that lists ships but not achievements
  • no documentary proof of rank time
  • no evidence of office skills such as reports, budgets or audits
  • applying for a role where the advert's essential qualification is a different CoC grade or degree
Key points
  • Most shore roles want a management-level CoC plus senior rank time; the exact bar is set by each employer.
  • In UPSC adverts from 2017 to 2022, Nautical Surveyor needed Master FG with one year in command, and Engineer & Ship Surveyor needed MEO Class I with one year as CE or 2E.
  • UPSC may shortlist above the minimum and rejects claims not backed by experience certificates, so check the current advert and your documents.
  • MTI faculty must meet STCW Section A-I/6 and complete DG Shipping's VICT (Model Course 6.09) unless they did TOTA/TOTI before 1 October 2018.
  • The ISM Code leaves DPA qualifications to the company, so audit training and fleet experience matter.
  • Each administration decides whether shore work counts as equivalent service for CoC revalidation, so confirm before your CoC expires.
  • Gather signed service testimonials and dry-dock or audit records while you are still at sea.

Frequently asked questions

How do I apply for a DG Shipping surveyor job?

DG Shipping surveyor posts are filled through Union Public Service Commission advertisements. You apply online on the UPSC portal against a specific vacancy. Adverts from 2017 to 2022 asked for a Master FG CoC with command time (nautical side) or MEO Class I with CE/2E time (engineering side), and the engineering recruitment rules were revised in 2025. Qualifications, age limits and closing dates can change with each advert, so read the current one.

Can a Second Engineer get a shore job without MEO Class I?

Yes. Possible roles include crewing, purchasing, fleet support, some junior superintendent posts, and instructor posts on courses whose guidelines accept your CoC level. However, government surveyor posts and most senior technical posts ask for Class I and CE or 2E time, so many engineers finish Class I before moving ashore.

Does working ashore count towards CoC revalidation?

It counts only if your administration accepts the job as equivalent to sea service under STCW Regulation I/11. Otherwise you will need qualifying sea time, an approved test or an approved course. Check with DG Shipping, MARINA or your flag administration well before your CoC expires.

What course do I need to teach at a maritime training institute in India?

DG Shipping's Training Manual requires principals, faculty and instructors at approved MTIs to complete the DG Shipping-approved Vertical Integration Course for Trainers (VICT), which is based on IMO Model Course 6.09. Faculty who completed TOTA/TOTI before 1 October 2018 are exempt. You also need the minimum CoC set in the DG Shipping guideline for the course you will teach.

Is a marine superintendent job only for Masters?

No convention rule covers this. It depends on the company. Many managers prefer Masters, but experienced Chief Officers are also hired, especially those with tanker vetting or cargo claims experience. Read the advert and show proof of the specific experience it asks for.

Test yourself

Under STCW, which provision sets the qualification standard for people who train and assess seafarers?

Regulation I/6 and Section A-I/6 cover training and assessment, including instructor and assessor qualifications. I/11 covers revalidation (STCW Code, Section A-I/6).

In UPSC adverts from 2017 to 2022, what experience did the DG Shipping Nautical Surveyor post require on top of the Master FG CoC?

The essential experience was five years as a deck officer after the Second Mate FG CoC, with one year as Master of a foreign-going ship (UPSC recruitment notices for DG Shipping).

A Chief Engineer has been a superintendent ashore for four years. What decides whether this counts towards CoC revalidation?

Section A-I/11 allows functions the administration considers equivalent to sea service, but STCW itself does not list which shore jobs qualify (STCW Code, Section A-I/11).

What does the ISM Code say about the qualifications of the Designated Person Ashore?

ISM Code clause 4 requires the company to designate a person with access to top management. Qualifications are left to the company, and guidance expects suitable training and experience (ISM Code, clause 4).

Which IMO model course is the VICT course, required by DG Shipping for MTI faculty, based on?

VICT is based on IMO Model Course 6.09, Training Course for Instructors; Model Course 3.12 covers assessment, examination and certification (DG Shipping's VICT guidelines, TR/CR 26 of 2018).
