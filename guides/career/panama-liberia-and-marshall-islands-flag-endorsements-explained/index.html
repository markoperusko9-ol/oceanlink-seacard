Panama, Liberia and Marshall Islands flag endorsements explained

Quick answer

Before you serve as an officer on a ship registered in Panama, Liberia or the Marshall Islands, you need that flag's endorsement recognising your national CoC under STCW Regulation I/10. Applications usually go through your company or an authorised filing agent, and the flag first checks with your issuing administration that your CoC is genuine. STCW Regulation I/10 allows, but does not oblige, a flag to let you serve for up to three months while your endorsement is processed if you carry documentary proof that you have applied, so check that the flag grants this before you join.

What a flag endorsement actually is

Your Certificate of Competency is issued by your own administration, such as DG Shipping in India or MARINA in the Philippines. When you join a ship flying another flag, that flag state has to formally accept your CoC. It does this with an endorsement attesting recognition under STCW Regulation I/10. Panama calls it a Certificate Endorsement or Title Endorsement. Liberia and the Marshall Islands (RMI) issue an Endorsement Certificate.

The principles are the same under all three flags because they come from the Convention:

  • A flag can only endorse certificates from countries it has recognised. It first checks the issuing country's training and certification system.
  • Before endorsing, the flag must confirm that your certificate is genuine and valid.
  • The endorsement cannot give you a higher capacity, or fewer limitations, than your original CoC.
  • The endorsement is only valid while your underlying CoC is valid. If your CoC expires, the endorsement goes with it.
  • You need a separate endorsement for each flag. A Panama endorsement does nothing for you on a Liberian ship.
  • Masters, chief mates, chief engineers and second engineers must show suitable knowledge of the flag state's maritime law for their duties. How each flag checks this is up to that flag.

Who needs one: officers, GMDSS, tanker and ratings

Certificate you hold Flag endorsement needed?
Officer CoC (deck, engine, ETO) Yes, under STCW I/10
GMDSS GOC/ROC Yes, usually as a separate GMDSS endorsement
Advanced tanker CoP for officers (oil, chemical, gas) Yes. Regulation I/10 covers these as well
Basic safety and other CoPs (PST, FPFF, PSCRB etc.) No I/10 endorsement. Panama has a separate "course endorsement" option, and the flag's own rules decide
Ratings (watch rating, Able Seafarer) No I/10 endorsement. The flag may issue its own Seafarer's Identification and Record Book (SIRB) or rating certificates, depending on its rules and your company

The exact list depends on the flag's own requirements and on the ship's Minimum Safe Manning Document. In practice, your company's crewing department tells you which endorsements to apply for. If you're joining a tanker, check that your tanker endorsement is included and not just your CoC.

Panama: Panama Maritime Authority (AMP)

Panama endorsements are issued by the General Directorate of Seafarers (DGGM) of the Panama Maritime Authority. You can apply at the AMP main office in Panama, at Panamanian consulates abroad, or through authorised brokers or companies.

The AMP checklists generally ask for:

  • The Application for Certificates and Endorsements for Seafarers, completed and signed
  • A valid passport (original)
  • A valid medical fitness certificate meeting STCW Regulation I/9 and MLC 2006
  • Your CoC and supporting certificates as listed on the checklist for your rank

Photo, form and document requirements are set out in the current AMP/DGGM checklist on amp.gob.pa. Follow it exactly.

The AMP's records cover officer certificate applications, certificate endorsements and course endorsements for CoPs.

Tip: many unofficial websites sell Panama endorsements. Use only the company's own channel or an agent your company names. Check the result through the verification facility linked from the official AMP website (amp.gob.pa). Never rely on a link someone sends you on WhatsApp.

Liberia: Liberia Maritime Authority via LISCR

Liberia's seafarer certification is administered through LISCR. The main requirements document is RLM-118, Requirements for Merchant Marine Personnel Certification. Points every officer should know:

  • Applications go through an authorised filing agent, usually arranged by your company or manning agent. Check the current RLM-118 for the filing rules.
  • Your CoC must be verified before it is sent to LISCR for endorsement. This means confirmation from your issuing administration.
  • Your medical certificate must meet Liberia's current time limits and be on the form RLM-118 specifies. Check the current RLM-118.
  • Equivalency endorsements are only available to seafarers from countries that Liberia recognises.
  • The Liberian SIRB holds your Liberian certificates. Sign your certificates and Special Qualification Certificates as soon as you receive them.
  • Under STCW I/10, recognition rests on the original CoC from the issuing country. An endorsement issued by another flag cannot itself be endorsed. Your Liberian endorsement, and any other flag's endorsement, must be based on your original national CoC.

Forms, revisions and fees change often. Always download the current RLM-118 from liscr.com instead of using an old copy saved on the ship's computer.

Marshall Islands: the RMI Maritime Administrator

The Republic of the Marshall Islands Maritime Administrator works through regional offices and authorised filing agents. Requirements are in MI-118, Requirements for Seafarer Certification, and the filing agent procedures are in MI-280.

  • Officer endorsements are applied for on form MI-105, Application for Endorsement of Officer Certificate of Competence. It is submitted through a regional office or an authorised filing agent.
  • On MI-105 the filing agent certifies that they have seen your original national CoC and verified it with the issuing administration. If your CoC cannot be verified, the application stops.
  • The filing agent checks that the application is complete and witnesses your signature. Missing or unclear supporting documents lead to rejection or delay.
  • Before applying for RMI seafarer documents, complete the consent form and SIRB application listed on register-iri.com. Your filing agent will tell you which ones apply.
  • The Marshall Islands issues a CRA (Certificate of Receipt of Application). Once a complete, signed MI-105 has been submitted, the CRA allows you to serve for up to 90 days while the endorsement is processed. Ask your filing agent how to verify your CRA, or use the verification options on register-iri.com.

Step-by-step: worked scenario

Scenario: an Indian 2nd Officer holding a DG Shipping CoC and a GMDSS GOC is appointed to a Liberian-flag product tanker. He joins in three weeks.

  1. Check what's needed. Crewing confirms he needs Liberian endorsements for his CoC, his GMDSS GOC and his tanker CoP. He also needs a Liberian SIRB.
  2. Gather documents. He collects clear colour scans of his CoC, GOC and tanker certificate, plus his passport, CDC and current medical. He also gets a photo to the specification in the checklist. Every document needs at least as much remaining validity as the company asks for.
  3. CoC verification. Before the file goes to LISCR, his CoC is verified with DG Shipping. If DG Shipping's records don't show the CoC as valid, the process stops here.
  4. Filing agent submits. The authorised filing agent arranged by his company lodges the application, following the current RLM-118 filing rules.
  5. Proof of application. He carries proof that the application has been submitted. If the flag allows it, he can serve for a limited period on this proof while the endorsement is processed (up to three months under STCW I/10).
  6. On board. The original endorsement reaches the ship or the agent. He checks his name, CoC number, capacity and limitations against his national CoC, then signs where required.
  7. Diary note. He notes the expiry dates of the endorsement and of his CoC. When he revalidates the CoC, he arranges a new endorsement before the old one expires.

Common rejections and how to avoid them

  • CoC cannot be verified. The issuing administration's online records don't match, or the CoC was recently revalidated and has not yet been uploaded. Check your own record on the DG Shipping or MARINA verification facility before you apply.
  • Name mismatch between passport, CoC, CDC and medical, such as initials, surname order or spelling. Fix this at the source, or attach the explanation your company asks for.
  • Capacity mismatch. For example, applying for Chief Mate when the CoC is for OOW, or for unlimited when the CoC has a tonnage, power or near-coastal limitation. The endorsement can't go beyond your original CoC.
  • Expired or expiring documents, such as a medical outside the flag's time limit, an expired GOC, or a tanker CoP not covered by recent service.
  • Poor scans: cropped, blurred, black-and-white or missing the reverse side.
  • Unsigned forms or wrong photo specification.
  • Old form versions. All three flags revise their forms. Use the current version from the official site.
  • Fake endorsements. These are bought through unofficial agents and then fail Port State Control checks. If anyone asks you for cash in return for a "guaranteed" endorsement, refuse and tell your company.

Oral exam corner: what examiners ask

This topic can come up in management-level oral exams. Be ready to answer the following:

  • Q: Under which STCW regulation does a flag recognise a foreign CoC? Regulation I/10, with the endorsement in the format set out in Regulation I/2.
  • Q: Can you sail on a Marshall Islands ship before your endorsement arrives? Only if the flag allows it. STCW I/10 lets a flag permit service for up to three months on documentary proof of application. The RMI does allow this: its CRA permits service for up to 90 days once a complete, signed MI-105 has been submitted.
  • Q: Your national CoC expires next month. What happens to your Panama endorsement? It stops being valid when the CoC expires. Revalidate the CoC first, then apply for a new endorsement.
  • Q: Why must a master know flag state law? Regulation I/10 requires management-level officers to have suitable knowledge of the flag's maritime law for their duties, for example logbook entries, reporting and crew matters.
  • Q: What will a PSC inspector check? The inspector checks that each officer's endorsement or valid proof of application matches the capacity they are serving in and the ship's safe manning document.
Key points
  • A flag endorsement is the flag state's recognition of your national CoC under STCW Regulation I/10.
  • You need a separate endorsement for each flag; Panama, Liberia and the Marshall Islands do not accept each other's endorsements.
  • Officer CoCs, GMDSS certificates and advanced tanker CoPs for officers all need a flag endorsement.
  • Your CoC is always verified with the issuing administration first, so make sure your online record is correct.
  • STCW I/10 allows, but does not oblige, a flag to let you serve up to three months on proof of application, so check the flag grants this before you join.
  • Liberia and Marshall Islands applications go through authorised filing agents or regional offices, usually arranged by your company.
  • An endorsement can never give you a higher capacity than your national CoC, and it ends when your CoC expires.

Frequently asked questions

Can I apply for a Liberian or Marshall Islands endorsement myself?

In practice, no. Liberian applications are handled through authorised filing agents, so check the current RLM-118 on liscr.com for the filing rules. The Marshall Islands routes applications through its regional offices and authorised filing agents. Your company or manning agent normally arranges both.

How long can I sail on a CRA or proof of application?

STCW Regulation I/10 allows, but does not oblige, a flag to let you serve for up to three months on documentary proof that your endorsement application has been submitted. The Marshall Islands issues a CRA allowing service for up to 90 days once a complete, signed MI-105 has been submitted. For other flags, check that this concession is granted before you join, and make sure the full endorsement arrives within the allowed period.

Do ratings need a Panama, Liberia or Marshall Islands endorsement?

Regulation I/10 endorsements apply to officers' CoCs and certain officer-level CoPs, not to rating certificates. However, these flags may require or issue their own seaman's book, SIRB or rating certificates. Your company will tell you what the ship needs.

Does my flag endorsement stay valid after I revalidate my CoC?

Your endorsement is valid only as long as the CoC it is based on, and it can never run past that CoC's expiry date. Once your national CoC is revalidated or reissued, often with a new number, apply for a new endorsement based on the new certificate. Do this before the old endorsement expires.

How do I know my Panama endorsement is genuine?

Apply only through your company or an agent your company names. Verify the document through the facility linked from the official Panama Maritime Authority website (amp.gob.pa). A fake endorsement will be found at PSC inspection and can lead to detention of the ship and loss of your job.

Test yourself

Which STCW regulation covers a flag state's recognition of a CoC issued by another Party?

Regulation I/10 covers recognition of certificates. I/9 is medical standards, I/11 is revalidation and I/14 is company responsibilities (STCW Convention, Annex, Regulation I/10).

An OOW holds a Liberian endorsement and is offered a Panama-flag ship. What does he need?

Each flag issues its own endorsement, which must be based on the original national CoC. An endorsement issued by another flag cannot itself be endorsed (STCW Regulation I/10; Liberia RLM-118).

Before LISCR endorses an officer's CoC, what must happen first?

Liberia requires CoCs to be verified before they are sent to LISCR for endorsement. STCW I/10 also requires the flag to confirm a certificate is genuine (Liberia RLM-118; STCW Regulation I/10).

A Chief Mate holds a national CoC with a near-coastal limitation. What can the flag endorsement show?

An endorsement attesting recognition cannot give a higher capacity or fewer limitations than the original certificate (STCW Regulation I/10).

Which form does the Marshall Islands use for an officer's application for endorsement of a CoC?

MI-105 is the officer endorsement application form, and MI-118 is the requirements document (RMI MI-118 and MI-280).
