Sea time verification and testimonials: how to make every day count

Quick answer

Your sea time only counts towards a CoC or revalidation if the administration can check it. For Indian seafarers that means sign-on and sign-off entries uploaded by a registered RPS agency to the DG Shipping e-governance system, backed by your CDC and the sea service letters or testimonials asked for at the MMD. For Filipino seafarers it means SIRB entries and a company-issued Certificate of Sea Service on MARINA's current template.

What verified sea time actually means

Every CoC under the STCW Convention depends on approved seagoing service, and every revalidation depends on recent seagoing service. Your administration has to be able to confirm three things: that you were on board, in what capacity, and on what kind of ship.

In practice the evidence comes from three places, and they must agree with each other:

  • The national database. In India this is the DG Shipping e-governance system and INDoS. In the Philippines it is MARINA's records, tied to your SIRB.
  • Your discharge book. This is the CDC (India) or the SIRB (Philippines), with sign-on and sign-off entries authenticated on board.
  • Letters and testimonials. These are the company's sea service letter or certificate, plus the master's testimonial or watchkeeping certificate where your administration asks for one.

Under MLC 2006, Standard A2.1, you are entitled to a record of your employment on board. That record must not contain any statement on the quality of your work or on your wages. MLC does not cover testimonials. Where an administration asks for an assessment of your conduct or ability, it comes in a separate master's testimonial.

The golden rule is simple. If the dates, rank or ship particulars differ between any of these sources, expect a query or a rejection at the counter.

India: how your sea service gets into the system

For Indian seafarers, the starting point is the online record, not the paper record.

  1. Join only through a registered RPS agency or company. Registered RPSL holders upload your sign-on and sign-off to DG Shipping's e-governance system. DG Shipping has warned that it cannot upload or consider service arranged through unregistered agents for exams or certification.
  2. Log in to your seafarer profile. Go to the DG Shipping website, open the e-governance link, log in and select View Sea Service and Acknowledge. DGS Crew Circular 25 of 2024 sets out this step.
  3. Check, then acknowledge. Check each sign-on and sign-off entry for vessel, IMO number, rank and dates. Acknowledge the entries if they are correct. If anything is wrong, do not acknowledge it. Raise it with your RPS agency using the procedure in the circular and the DGS FAQ on Crew Matters.
  4. Check it every contract, not every five years. Look at your profile in the same month you sign on and sign off. Errors are much easier to fix while the agency still has your paperwork to hand.
  5. Keep your CDC entries clean. Only the master or an authorised person should authenticate entries, with the ship's stamp and no overwriting. In 2025 DG Shipping published a draft circular on preventing forged CDC entries. Check dgshipping.gov.in to see whether a final version has been issued.

If an entry is wrong or missing, see the DGS FAQ on Crew Matters for the current correction procedure. Do not try to get the CDC overwritten on board.

Note: Exam applications are made online through the DG Shipping e-governance system. Portal names and menus change, so check the current official DG Shipping site.

India: what the MMD checks at exam time

Before you can sit a CoC exam, your sea service goes through two checks: one online and one on paper.

  • Your eligibility depends on the sea service and course records held against your INDoS number. These are assessed against the STCW sea service and course requirements.
  • After the online eligibility check, take the generated application and your originals to the MMD.
  • The MMD checks your originals against the online record. These can include sea service letters, training certificates, your medical certificate and your CDC.
  • If any of your service is backed by papers issued abroad, ask the MMD what verification or endorsement it needs before you apply.

MMDs have also asked for an original sea service letter from the company on RPSL letterhead, and a master's testimonial in the DGS watchkeeping certificate format. Checklists change, so download the current list from the MMD or DG Shipping site rather than relying on a senior's old file.

If the online check says you are not eligible, the cause is almost always the data, not the rules. A missing sign-off, a rank uploaded as "Trainee" instead of "Third Officer", or the wrong engine power will all cut your counted days.

Philippines: SIRB entries and the Certificate of Sea Service

For Filipino seafarers, the two key documents are your SIRB and a Certificate of Sea Service issued by the company or manning agency.

The MARINA STCW Office publishes a template for the Certificate of Sea Service. Always use the current version. Make sure that it:

  • carries your SIRB number
  • lists each period of seagoing service with the vessel, rank and dates
  • bears the company seal
  • has no erasures or alterations

Check the current template for the exact requirements.

For CoC revalidation, check the current MARINA circular on certification under the STCW Convention. It lists the documents you need to show that you meet the recent service requirement in STCW Section A-I/11, or an accepted equivalent. Expect to need company-attested proof of your seagoing service.

Steps that work:

  1. Before you sign off, make sure the master has authenticated your SIRB entry.
  2. Ask your manning agency for the Certificate of Sea Service on the current MARINA template. Check it against the SIRB before you leave the office.
  3. If the current circular asks for a job description, get one that matches the functions in your CoC: navigation, cargo handling or engineering watch.
  4. Check the latest requirements and advisories on the MARINA STCW website. MARINA issues advisories often.

DMW deals with deployment and contracts, not with counting sea service. Take sea time questions to MARINA.

A testimonial that will not bounce: checklist

Whatever your flag or administration, a sea service letter or testimonial should show the following:

Item Why it matters
Full name exactly as in passport and CDC/SIRB Name mismatches are a frequent reason for queries
CDC/SIRB number and INDoS (India) Links the letter to the database record
Ship name, IMO number, flag, GT GT limits apply to many CoCs
Main engine power (kW) for engineers Engineering CoCs are limited by propulsion power
Ship type (oil, chemical, gas, bulk, etc.) Needed for tanker endorsements
Trading area (e.g. foreign-going or near-coastal) Near-coastal service may count differently
Rank and exact dates of sign-on and sign-off Must match the uploaded data to the day
Watchkeeping hours or duties performed Many CoCs need watchkeeping, not just time on board
Master's signature, name and ship's stamp; company seal on company letters Unstamped or unsigned letters are rejected
No overwriting or correction fluid Alterations make the document invalid

Tips from the exam room:

  • Get the testimonial signed before you leave the ship. Chasing a master who has gone home months later rarely works.
  • Keep scans of every letter, CDC page and passport stamp in one folder.
  • For cadets, the TAR book is separate evidence and does not replace sea service letters. The training record and the sea time must agree with each other.

Common rejections, with worked scenarios

Scenario 1: dates one day out. The CDC shows sign-off on the 14th, the RPSL upload says the 15th, and the testimonial says the 14th. The MMD flags the mismatch.

Fix: Before you apply, ask the agency to correct the upload through the official route. Then acknowledge the corrected entry in your profile.

Scenario 2: wrong rank. You were promoted on board mid-contract, but the upload shows the whole contract in your old rank. Your days as a watchkeeper are short.

Fix: Get a master's letter and a company letter showing the promotion date, plus a corrected upload. Service in the lower rank may not count towards the higher CoC.

Scenario 3: unregistered agent. Your service was on a foreign-flag ship you joined through an unregistered agent. Nothing appears in your profile.

Fix: There may not be a clean fix. Contact the MMD with all your evidence and be ready for the service not to count. Check an agency's licence before you sign anything.

Scenario 4: missing GT or kW. An engineer's letter leaves out the main engine power, so the examiner cannot apply the power limit.

Fix: Get the letter reissued on company letterhead with the ship particulars.

Other regular problems:

  • letters with no seal
  • photocopies offered as originals
  • a name spelt differently from the passport
  • yard, standby or lay-up periods counted as seagoing service when your administration does not accept them
  • service on vessels outside the trading area or size range for the certificate

How time in port, at anchor or in dry dock is counted depends on the administration. Check the rule; do not assume.

Oral exam style: questions examiners actually ask

Q: Who certifies your sea service? The master authenticates the discharge entry on board. The company or agency issues the sea service letter, and in India the RPS agency uploads the dates to the e-governance system. The administration then checks that these all agree.

Q: What is the recent service requirement for revalidating a CoC? STCW Section A-I/11 accepts any one of the following:

  • at least 12 months' approved seagoing service in total during the preceding five years
  • 3 months in the preceding six months
  • equivalent functions
  • an approved test or course
  • 3 months as a supernumerary or in a lower rank

Q: Can your discharge book entry say you were an excellent officer? No. Under MLC Standard A2.1 the record of employment must not contain any statement on the quality of your work or on your wages. MLC does not cover testimonials. Where an administration asks for an assessment of conduct or ability, it comes in a separate master's testimonial.

Q: You find a wrong date in your online profile after signing off. What do you do? Do not acknowledge the entry. Raise it with your RPS agency so it can be corrected through the procedure in DGS Crew Circular 25 of 2024 and the DGS FAQ on Crew Matters. Keep copies of your CDC and letters, and do not apply for the exam until the record is right.

Q: Why does ship type matter on the letter? Tanker endorsements and some CoCs need service on particular ship types, sizes or engine powers. The examiner cannot assume what the letter does not say.

Key points
  • Sea time counts only if the database, your CDC/SIRB and your letters all agree to the day.
  • Indian seafarers should open View Sea Service and Acknowledge in the DG Shipping e-governance profile every time they sign on and sign off.
  • Join only through a registered RPS agency, or your service may never reach the system.
  • In India, after the online eligibility check, take the generated application and your originals to the MMD; check the current checklist first.
  • Filipino seafarers need a Certificate of Sea Service on MARINA's current template, sealed by the company and free of alterations.
  • Every letter should state the ship's IMO number, GT, kW, type, rank and exact dates.
  • Get testimonials signed before you leave the ship and keep scans of everything.

Frequently asked questions

How do I check my sea service on the DG Shipping website?

Log in to your seafarer profile through the e-governance link on the DG Shipping website and open View Sea Service and Acknowledge. There you can see the sign-on and sign-off entries uploaded by your RPS agency. Acknowledge them if they are correct; if anything is wrong, do not acknowledge it and raise it with your agency. Menus change, so follow the current user manual on the official site.

My RPSL agency uploaded the wrong sign-off date. How do I correct it?

Do not acknowledge the wrong entry in your profile. Ask the agency to correct it through the official route. See the DGS FAQ on Crew Matters for the current correction procedure, and keep CDC copies and sea service letters ready as supporting evidence.

Is a master's testimonial alone enough for a CoC exam in India?

No. The MMD checks the testimonial against the sea service uploaded to your online record and against your CDC. A testimonial that does not match the online record will usually be queried. Download the current document checklist from the MMD or DG Shipping site.

What does MARINA need as proof of sea service?

MARINA relies on your SIRB entries and a Certificate of Sea Service issued by the company on the current STCW Office template. Make sure the certificate carries the company seal and has no erasures or alterations. For revalidation, check the current MARINA circular and advisories for the full document list.

Does time in dry dock or at anchor count as sea time?

It depends on the administration and on the certificate you are applying for, so there is no single answer. Some administrations count certain periods in full, some count them in part, and some do not count them at all. Check the current rules of your administration before you rely on such periods.

Test yourself

Which DG Shipping e-governance option lets an Indian seafarer confirm the sign-on and sign-off details uploaded by the agency?

DGS Crew Circular 25 of 2024 tells seafarers to use View Sea Service and Acknowledge to check and acknowledge their uploaded sign-on and sign-off details.

Under MLC 2006, what must the record of employment given to a seafarer NOT contain?

MLC 2006 Standard A2.1 says the record of employment must not contain any statement on the quality of the seafarer's work or on their wages.

What does STCW Section A-I/11 normally require in seagoing service for revalidating a CoC?

STCW Code Section A-I/11 sets at least 12 months of seagoing service in total during the preceding five years, with alternatives for those who fall short.

Under STCW Section A-I/11, which of these is an accepted alternative to 12 months' seagoing service in the previous five years?

STCW Code Section A-I/11 accepts 3 months' approved seagoing service as a supernumerary officer, or in a rank lower than the certificate is valid for, as one of the alternatives.

Your DG Shipping profile shows a wrong sign-off date. What should you do?

Under DGS Crew Circular 25 of 2024 you check and acknowledge entries only if they are correct. Errors are corrected through the RPS agency using the procedure in the DGS FAQ on Crew Matters.
