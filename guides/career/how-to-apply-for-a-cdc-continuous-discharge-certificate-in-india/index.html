How to Apply for a CDC (Continuous Discharge Certificate) in India

Quick answer

You apply for an Indian CDC online through the DG Shipping e-Governance system, using your INDoS-linked seafarer profile. You will need your pre-sea and STCW certificates, a medical from a DGS-approved doctor and your identity documents. The CDC is issued by the Shipping Master at a Government Shipping Office (Mumbai, Chennai or Kolkata), and under the current CDC Rules it is valid for ten years. Eligibility rules are being tightened, so check the DG Shipping Crew Division page before you pay for any course or application.

What the Indian CDC is, and what it is not

The Continuous Discharge Certificate is the booklet that records your sea service. Every sign-on and sign-off is entered and stamped in it. Crewing agencies and the MMD use it as the main record of your sea service.

The issuing authority is the Shipping Master, Government Shipping Office (GSO), at Mumbai, Chennai or Kolkata. The GSO is an allied office of the Directorate General of Shipping.

Two points that confuse many applicants:

  • CDC is not a CoC. The CDC records your service. A Certificate of Competency is your qualification to serve as an officer or engineer. Officers need both.
  • CDC and SID are being separated. Older booklets were printed as "CDC-cum-SID". A draft DGS Crew Branch circular (February 2025) says those booklets would be treated only as a CDC, for recording sea service and voyage details. The identity function would belong to the separate biometric SID issued under ILO Convention 185. Check the DG Shipping Crew Division page for the final position.

The current legal basis is the Merchant Shipping (Continuous Discharge Certificate) Rules, 2017, which replaced the 2001 CDC-cum-SID Rules. DG Shipping has also published draft CDC Rules, 2026 under the new Merchant Shipping Act, 2025. Expect the rule numbers and some wording to change once those rules are notified.

Who is eligible: the routes to a CDC

The CDC Rules, 2017 and related DGS Orders set the eligibility conditions. The DG Shipping CDC page confirms that you must meet the medical fitness standard under the Merchant Shipping (Medical Examination) Rules, 2000 and complete the basic STCW courses. Other conditions depend on the category you are applying in. The DG Shipping CDC page links to the rules and to the DGS Orders that modify them. Read that page rather than relying on a training institute's brochure.

The most common route is a DGS-approved pre-sea course plus the basic STCW courses. This covers deck and engine cadets (DNS, B.Sc, GME), GP Ratings and saloon ratings. Other categories may have different permitted routes. Check the DGS orders and the CDC page for the routes that apply to you.

Watch this change. DG Shipping has circulated a draft order on improving rating standards. It proposes to stop issuing CDCs on the strength of the five basic STCW courses alone (PST, FPFF, EFA, PSSR and STSDSD) with no approved pre-sea course behind them. Under the draft, a CDC would only be issued through the permitted routes listed in the draft order (see the DGS order). Institutes would also have to stop advertising the five courses as a standalone way to get a CDC. Applications already submitted before the order is issued would be processed under the existing rules.

The draft proposes that the order take effect from 1 April 2026, and it invited stakeholder comments. Check whether it has been finalised.

Until the order is final, do not pay any agent or institute promising a "CDC package" from short courses alone. Check the Crew Division page first.

Before you start: INDoS profile and document checklist

The CDC application pulls your personal data from your INDoS profile on the DG Shipping e-Governance system. If the profile is wrong, the application will be wrong. Your INDoS number is normally generated through the training institute where you did your first DGS-approved course.

Get these ready as clean colour scans. The portal shows the exact list, file formats and size limits when you start.

  • Proof of Indian citizenship. A passport is the usual document.
  • Proof of educational qualification. At minimum the 10th standard pass certificate or marksheet, plus higher qualifications if your category needs them.
  • Medical fitness certificate from a DGS-approved doctor, in the prescribed form, and still in date.
  • Pre-sea training course completion certificate from a DGS-approved institute, where your category needs one.
  • Basic STCW course certificates.
  • Passport-size photograph and scanned signature that meet the portal's specifications. Use the same photo and signature as your INDoS profile.
  • The signed online application. The portal generates it for you to sign and upload.

Tip: use one name, one spelling and one date of birth across everything. A missing middle name or swapped initials between your passport, 10th certificate and course certificates is a frequent reason for a query.

Step-by-step: applying online

DG Shipping is moving to a new website, so menu labels may differ slightly from what is shown below. The sequence stays the same.

  1. Log in to the DG Shipping e-Governance system with your seafarer user ID and password. This is the account linked to your INDoS number.
  2. Update your profile first. Check your name, date of birth, address, photo, signature, education and course details against the originals. Correct any errors before you start the CDC form.
  3. Open the CDC module. On the current e-Governance system it sits under CDC management. Select a fresh CDC application.
  4. Choose your category (for example deck, engine or saloon rating, cadet, or officer). Then select the Shipping Office from the options the portal offers. Fresh CDC processing has historically been centralised at the Shipping Master, Mumbai (DGS Order No. 2 of 2008), so follow the portal.
  5. Fill in the form. Personal details come pre-filled from INDoS. You enter your address, medical certificate details and course details.
  6. Upload your documents in the format the portal asks for.
  7. Pay the fee online. The DG Shipping CDC page lists one non-refundable fee for issue, reissue, renewal, replacement or duplicate. It was INR 700 when this guide was written. Check the current figure on the official page before paying.
  8. Submit and track the application from your login. Answer any query raised by the Shipping Office quickly, through the portal.
  9. Collect or receive the CDC in whatever way the Shipping Office tells you. Check every printed detail as soon as you have the booklet.

Pay only through the official portal. No legitimate step needs cash to an agent.

Renewal, replacement and duplicate CDC

Under the CDC Rules, 2017, a CDC is valid for ten years. You can renew it on expiry, or within one year before the expiry date. DG Shipping separates three services, and choosing the wrong one wastes time:

  • Renewal extends validity. You can apply on expiry or within one year before the expiry date. If your next contract will run past expiry, apply within that window before joining.
  • Replacement is for a CDC that is defaced, mutilated, mislaid or destroyed, or whose pages are full. Apply before the last page is used, not after. The draft 2026 Rules require the existing CDC to be surrendered before a replacement is issued.
  • Duplicate is for a lost CDC. You must attach a copy of the police FIR.

Keep a full colour scan of every page of your CDC, including all sign-on and sign-off entries, in your email or cloud storage. If the booklet is lost on a ship or in transit, those scans and your company service letters make the duplicate application and later sea-time verification much easier.

Common rejections and queries, and how to avoid them

These issues commonly cause queries:

  • Name or date of birth mismatch between your INDoS profile, passport, 10th certificate and course certificates. Fix the profile, or get the certificate corrected by the issuing institute, before you apply.
  • Medical certificate problems. The certificate is not from a DGS-approved doctor, is in the wrong form, or expires during processing.
  • Course certificates that will not verify online. STCW certificates are checked against the institute's uploads to DG Shipping. If a certificate cannot be verified, contact the institute. The Shipping Office cannot fix it for you.
  • Ineligible route. The applicant has only short courses and no approved pre-sea training, in a category or period where that is no longer accepted.
  • Poor scans. Uploads are blurred, cropped or black-and-white, or the photo does not meet the portal's specifications.
  • Wrong service requested. Someone asks for a duplicate when the booklet is only full, or for renewal when the pages have run out.
  • Applying for a second CDC. You may hold only one Indian CDC. Declaring otherwise is a serious matter.

Worked scenarios

Scenario 1: GP Rating graduate. Ravi finishes a DGS-approved GP Rating course and the basic STCW courses, and has a medical from a DGS-approved doctor. His institute generated his INDoS number. He logs in and finds his course certificates already showing. He corrects a misspelt surname so it matches his passport. He then applies under the rating category, selects the Shipping Office the portal offers and pays online. The office raises a query about a blurred 10th marksheet, and he clears it by uploading a fresh colour scan.

Scenario 2: A "CDC in 15 days" offer. Priya is offered a CDC by an agent if she does the five basic courses and pays a lump sum. She has done no pre-sea course. She checks the DG Shipping Crew Division page and sees the draft order to stop five-course-only CDCs. She also checks the institute's approval on DGS. She walks away and looks at approved pre-sea courses instead.

Scenario 3: Pages full mid-career. An AB with eight years left on his CDC has two blank pages and is joining for a nine-month contract. He applies for a replacement, not a renewal, before joining. The service record in the old booklet stays valid as part of his history.

Scenario 4: Fake stamp. A friend offers to "add three months" to a cadet's CDC through an RPSL contact. DG Shipping has issued a circular against forged CDC entries. RPSL agencies or companies involved face suspension or cancellation of their licence. Never accept a sea-service entry for time you did not serve.

Oral exam angle: what you should be able to explain

Examiners at MMD orals and company interviewers often test whether you understand your own documents. Be ready to answer these without notes:

  • Who issues the CDC? The Shipping Master of the Government Shipping Office (Mumbai, Chennai or Kolkata), under DG Shipping.
  • What is its validity? Ten years under the 2017 Rules, renewable on expiry or within one year before it.
  • What is the difference between CDC and SID? The CDC records sea service. The SID is the separate identity document under ILO C185. A draft DGS circular (February 2025) treats old CDC-cum-SID booklets only as a CDC. Check the Crew Division page for the final position.
  • Can anyone add sea time to your CDC? No. Entries must reflect service actually done on board. DG Shipping has issued a circular against forged entries, and RPSL agencies or companies involved face suspension or cancellation of their licence.
  • What do you do if it is lost at sea? Report it to the Master and the company, file an FIR, and apply online for a duplicate with the FIR copy.
  • Why does it matter for your CoC? Sea time for CoC eligibility is checked against CDC entries and supporting testimonials, so gaps and errors cause delays later.
Key points
  • Apply for a fresh CDC online through the DG Shipping e-Governance system, using your INDoS-linked profile.
  • The CDC is issued by the Shipping Master at a Government Shipping Office (Mumbai, Chennai or Kolkata); select the office the portal offers.
  • Under the CDC Rules, 2017 a CDC is valid for ten years and can be renewed on expiry or within one year before it.
  • Renewal, replacement (pages full or damaged) and duplicate (lost, with an FIR) are separate services.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get an Indian CDC with only the five basic STCW courses?

DG Shipping has circulated a draft order to stop issuing CDCs on the basis of the five basic STCW courses alone. Under the draft, you would need to qualify through one of the permitted routes listed in the order, such as an approved pre-sea course. The draft proposes a start date of 1 April 2026. Check the DG Shipping Crew Division CDC page for the current position before you pay any institute or agent.

Do I need an INDoS number before applying for a CDC?

Yes. The CDC application runs through your seafarer profile on the DG Shipping e-Governance system, and that profile is linked to your INDoS number. Your personal details are pulled from the profile, so correct any errors in it before you apply.

How long is an Indian CDC valid?

Under the Merchant Shipping (CDC) Rules, 2017, a CDC is valid for ten years. You can renew it on expiry or within one year before the expiry date. Do not let it run out partway through a contract.

What should I do if my CDC is lost or the pages are full?

If the CDC is lost, file a police FIR and apply online for a duplicate CDC with a copy of the FIR. If the pages are full or the booklet is damaged, apply for a replacement CDC instead. Both are applied for online through DG Shipping.

Is my old CDC-cum-SID still valid as an identity document?

A draft DG Shipping Crew Branch circular (February 2025) says old booklets printed as CDC-cum-SID would be treated only as a CDC, for recording sea service and voyage details. Identity would be covered by the separate Seafarer Identity Document issued under ILO Convention 185. Check the DG Shipping Crew Division page for the final position.
