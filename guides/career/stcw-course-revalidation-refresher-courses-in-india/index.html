STCW course revalidation (refresher courses) in India: a practical guide

Quick answer

In India, PST, FPFF, PSCRB, AFF and (if you hold it) PFRB need a DG Shipping-approved refresher at intervals of no more than five years, completed before each certificate's five-year interval runs out. STCW itself has no five-yearly refresher for Medical First Aid or Medical Care, but DG Shipping has its own additional refresher mandates and approved Refresher MFA and Refresher Medical Care courses, so Indian holders should expect to need them. CoC revalidation is a separate process with its own Refresher and Updating Training (RUT) course, so do not confuse the two.

Which STCW courses need a refresher, and how often

Since the 2010 Manila amendments, STCW expects seafarers to show every five years that they still meet the standard of competence in the basic safety, survival craft and fire-fighting proficiencies. DG Shipping applies this through its circulars. The courses that need a refresher are:

Original course Refresher you book Interval
Personal Survival Techniques (PST) Refresher PST Not more than 5 years
Fire Prevention and Fire Fighting (FPFF) Refresher FPFF Not more than 5 years
Proficiency in Survival Craft and Rescue Boats other than fast rescue boats (PSCRB) Refresher PSCRB Not more than 5 years
Proficiency in Fast Rescue Boats (PFRB) Refresher PFRB Not more than 5 years (if you hold PFRB)
Advanced Fire Fighting (AFF) Refresher AFF Not more than 5 years

Some points the candidates I examine regularly get wrong:

  • Medical First Aid and Medical Care: STCW itself has no five-yearly refresher for EFA, MFA or Medical Care. However, DG Shipping has its own additional refresher mandates. DGS-approved Refresher MFA and Refresher Medical Care courses exist and are named in Training Circular 4 of 2025. If you hold MFA or Medical Care, expect to need the DGS refresher, and check the current DGS revalidation checklist.
  • PSSR and Security Awareness have no five-yearly refresher requirement under STCW.
  • Tanker certificates of proficiency (oil, chemical, gas) follow different rules. These are covered in a section below.
  • The refresher is linked to your own certificate's date, not to a fixed national date. Each refresher must be completed before that certificate's five-year interval runs out. Your PST and your PSCRB can fall due at different times.

Refresher courses vs CoC revalidation (RUT): don't mix them up

Officers most often get confused here.

  • Refresher courses (PST, FPFF, PSCRB, PFRB, AFF, and DGS's MFA/Medical Care refreshers) keep your certificates of proficiency valid. Ratings, cadets and officers all need the ones that match the certificates they hold.
  • Refresher and Updating Training (RUT) is the course officers do to revalidate the Certificate of Competency every five years.

The DGS examination FAQs make three points:

  1. For CoC revalidation, DGS does not require you to repeat the PST, FPFF, PSCRB and AFF refreshers if you did them within the last five years.
  2. A CoC is normally revalidated for five years from the date you complete the RUT course.
  3. If you apply for revalidation within six months before the CoC expires, the new validity normally runs for five years from the old expiry date.

DGS also says the RUT course is not the same as the old 'revalidation course'. Earlier tanker courses do not have to be repeated just because you are doing RUT. Revalidating the CoC itself is covered in a separate guide.

Step-by-step: getting your refreshers done in India

  1. List your certificates and their issue dates. Write the issue date of each of PST, FPFF, PSCRB, AFF, and PFRB, MFA or Medical Care if you hold them, and add five years. That gives your deadline for each refresher. Treat the earliest one as your planning date.
  2. Check your INDoS record. Course certificates issued by DGS-approved institutes are uploaded against your INDoS number. Use the certificate-verification facility on the DG Shipping website (dgshipping.gov.in, also published under dgma.gov.in) to confirm that every original certificate shows up correctly. Use whichever official portal is current. If one is missing, sort it out with the institute that issued it before you book anything.
  3. Choose a DGS-approved Maritime Training Institute (MTI). Only courses at DGS-approved institutes, approved for that specific refresher course, are accepted. DGS publishes the list of approved institutes. Do not rely on an agent's word.
  4. Book and attend. Take your originals and photocopies (see the next section). Refreshers are mostly practical: donning lifejackets and immersion suits, survival-craft drills, firefighting with breathing apparatus. Expect to get wet and dirty. Check the course length with the institute, because it is set by the DGS-approved course.
  5. Exit exam. Training Circular 4 of 2025 (5 Feb 2025) discontinued exit exams for the refreshers in PST, FPFF, PSCRB, AFF, Medical Care, Medical First Aid and the Refresher and Updating Training course for engineers. Institutes conduct internal assessments instead. DGS later issued Training Circular 22 of 2025 (13 May 2025) on a revised exit-exam framework for MTIs. Ask your institute which rule applies on the date you attend.
  6. Collect and verify the certificate. Before you leave the institute, check your name, date of birth, INDoS number and course name. A few days later, check that the refresher certificate is listed against your INDoS on the DGS verification page (DG Shipping website, also published under dgma.gov.in; use whichever official portal is current).
  7. Update your documents. Give scanned copies to your crewing agency or manning office and keep the originals in your document folder for sign-on.

Documents to carry and common reasons for rejection

Typical documents institutes ask for (exact lists vary by institute, so confirm when you book):

  • Passport, plus the CDC if you hold one
  • INDoS number
  • Original certificate of the course you are refreshing, and any earlier refresher certificate
  • Valid seafarer medical fitness certificate, especially for refreshers with water or fire-ground practicals
  • Passport-size photographs

Problems examiners, institutes and crewing offices see repeatedly:

  • Name mismatch between passport, CDC, INDoS and the course certificate (initials, spelling, surname order). Fix it at source through the proper DGS route. It will block your CoC application later.
  • Original certificate not in the DGS database. This happens with old certificates or institutes that have since closed. Sort it out before booking the refresher.
  • Institute or course not approved by DGS. The certificate is useless for Indian documentation, and foreign flags and port State inspectors may question it.
  • Letting it lapse while on board. Port State Control can query out-of-date proficiencies, and many companies will not let you sign on with less than a set number of months of validity left.
  • Booking the wrong course. For example, booking a fast rescue boat refresher when you need PSCRB, forgetting AFF when you hold an officer's CoC, or skipping the DGS Refresher MFA or Medical Care when you hold those certificates.

Worked scenarios

Scenario 1: Third Officer with staggered certificates PST and FPFF were issued in mid-2020, PSCRB in early 2021 and AFF in late 2021. The officer is home in early 2025 for three months and the next contract will run past mid-2025. Action: Do the PST and FPFF refreshers now, because they fall due during the next contract. PSCRB and AFF also fall due within about a year, and most seafarers book all four together to avoid a gap. Doing them early is allowed. Each new refresher certificate restarts that course's five-year interval.

Scenario 2: Chief Officer revalidating the CoC The CoC expires in four months. PST, FPFF, PSCRB and AFF were done six years ago. PSSR and MFA are older still. Action: Do the RUT course and the four refreshers, then apply for revalidation within the six-month window. PSSR does not need a refresher. MFA has no STCW refresher, but DG Shipping has its own additional refresher mandates, so expect to need the DGS Refresher MFA, and Refresher Medical Care if you hold Medical Care. Check the current DGS revalidation checklist.

Scenario 3: AB coming back after a long break ashore The AB holds PST, FPFF, PSCRB and EFA from seven years ago. Action: Do the PST, FPFF and PSCRB refreshers before applying for jobs. Crewing agencies screen for this first. EFA does not need an STCW refresher.

Filipino seafarers: the STCW interval is the same, but approvals, accredited training centres and certificate verification go through MARINA. Check MARINA's current issuances rather than using Indian DGS rules.

Tanker certificates: a different rule

STCW Regulation V/1-1 and V/1-2 certificates of proficiency (oil/chemical and liquefied gas) are not covered by the PST/FPFF-style refresher system.

Under STCW Regulation I/11 and Section A-I/11, masters and officers must show continued professional competence for tankers at intervals of no more than five years. They can do this either with approved tanker sea service of at least three months in total in the previous five years or by completing an approved relevant training course.

The DGS FAQs deal with the common case of an officer who has been on containers or bulkers for five years and wants to go back to tankers. The administration decides the exact route, whether a specified course or proof of tanker service. Indian endorsement (DCE) rules also have their own conditions. Check the current DGS requirements before you take a tanker contract.

Oral exam corner: what an MMD surveyor may ask

At revalidation or at a competency oral, expect quick checks like these:

  • "Which of your certificates need a refresher, and when is each one due?" Give the date of every certificate from memory. Saying "the agent handles it" does not go down well.
  • "Is RUT the same as a refresher?" No. RUT is for revalidating the CoC. The PST, FPFF, PSCRB, PFRB and AFF refreshers are for the proficiencies.
  • "Does Medical First Aid need a refresher?" Not under STCW, which has no five-yearly refresher for EFA, MFA or Medical Care. However, DG Shipping separately mandates Refresher MFA and Refresher Medical Care for Indian certificate holders, so say that you follow the current DGS requirement.
  • "You have had no tanker service for five years. Can you join a tanker as an officer on your old tanker CoP?" Not without re-establishing competence under STCW Section A-I/11, either through tanker service in the last five years or an approved course, as the administration accepts.
  • "What did the PSCRB refresher cover?" Launching and recovery, taking charge of survival craft, and using equipment. Be ready to describe a davit-launched lifeboat drill step by step.

The practical tip is to keep a single sheet in your document folder listing every certificate with its number, issue date and due date. Examiners, PSC inspectors and crewing staff all notice it.

Key points
  • PST, FPFF, PSCRB, AFF and (if held) PFRB need a DGS-approved refresher at intervals of no more than five years.
  • Complete each refresher before that certificate's five-year interval runs out; for CoC revalidation, refreshers done within the last five years need not be repeated.
  • STCW has no five-yearly refresher for MFA or Medical Care, but DG Shipping mandates its own Refresher MFA and Medical Care courses for Indian holders.
  • RUT revalidates the CoC. It does not replace the proficiency refreshers.
  • Training Circular 4 of 2025 discontinued exit exams for the PST, FPFF, PSCRB, AFF, MFA, Medical Care and engineers' RUT refreshers; check the current rule with your institute.
  • Before and after the course, confirm on the official DG Shipping verification facility that every certificate is listed against your INDoS.
  • Tanker proficiencies have their own route: three months' tanker service in five years or an approved course.

Frequently asked questions

How often do I need to do STCW refresher courses in India?

PST, FPFF, PSCRB, AFF and, if you hold it, PFRB need a refresher at intervals of no more than five years. The clock runs from the date of your last certificate for each course, so different courses can fall due at different times. Complete each refresher before its five-year interval runs out.

Do I need to repeat refresher courses for CoC revalidation if I did them recently?

No. For CoC revalidation, DG Shipping does not require you to repeat the PST, FPFF, PSCRB and AFF refreshers if you did them within the last five years. You still need the RUT course for the CoC itself.

Does Medical First Aid need revalidation every five years?

Not under STCW, which has no five-yearly refresher for MFA or Medical Care. However, DG Shipping has its own additional refresher mandates, and DGS-approved Refresher MFA and Refresher Medical Care courses are named in Training Circular 4 of 2025. Indian holders should expect to need them, so check the current DGS revalidation checklist.

Is there an exit exam for refresher courses?

DGS Training Circular 4 of 2025 (5 Feb 2025) discontinued exit exams for the refreshers in PST, FPFF, PSCRB, AFF, Medical Care, Medical First Aid and the engineers' Refresher and Updating Training course, with institutes conducting internal assessments instead. DGS has since issued Training Circular 22 of 2025 on a revised exit-exam framework for training institutes, so confirm the current position with your institute when you book.

How do I check that my refresher certificate is genuine and recorded?

Use the certificate-verification facility on the DG Shipping website (also published under dgma.gov.in) with your INDoS number, using whichever official portal is current. If the course does not appear, contact the institute that issued it straight away, because unrecorded certificates cause problems at CoC applications and with crewing agencies.

Test yourself

Your PSCRB was issued three years ago and your PST six years ago. Which refresher do you need now?

Each course has its own interval of no more than five years. The PST is overdue, and the PSCRB is still within its interval (STCW Code, Sections A-VI/1 and A-VI/2; DGS examination FAQs).

Which of these does NOT need five-yearly revalidation under STCW 2010 as amended?

STCW has no five-yearly refresher requirement for Medical First Aid, though DG Shipping separately mandates a refresher MFA for Indian certificate holders (STCW Code, Section A-VI/4; DGS Training Circular 4 of 2025).

What does the Refresher and Updating Training (RUT) course do?

RUT supports CoC revalidation under STCW Regulation I/11. The proficiency refreshers are a separate requirement (DGS examination FAQs).

An officer with no tanker service in the last five years wants to rejoin tankers. What does STCW require?

Continued professional competence for tankers is set out in STCW Regulation I/11 and Section A-I/11, paragraph 3.

An Indian officer applies for CoC revalidation four months before the CoC expires. From when does the new five-year validity normally run?

DGS FAQs say that when an officer applies within six months before expiry, the CoC is normally revalidated for five years from the old expiry date.
