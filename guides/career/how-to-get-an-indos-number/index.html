How to Get an INDoS Number: Step-by-Step for Indian Seafarers

Quick answer

You can't apply for an INDoS number directly as a first-timer. You register online with DG Shipping and get a reference number, which is valid for three months. Then you go in person to a DGS-approved Maritime Training Institute (MTI) with your original passport and Class 10 marksheet, and the institute generates the number. The number is permanent, it goes on every certificate you hold, and you need it before you can join any DGS-approved course.

What the INDoS number is and why you can't sail without it

INDoS stands for the Indian National Database of Seafarers. The Directorate General of Shipping (DGS) issues the number, and the INDoS work sits under the DGS Maritime Training Trust (MTT). The database exists to stop people using fraudulent certificates. It also helps India meet its STCW obligation to share certificate information. Flag states, port states, immigration officers and employers can all use it to check your documents.

The number has eight characters and never changes. For example, in 18NM1234:

  • The first two digits are the year the number was issued.
  • The third and fourth characters are letters that show your category. Codes include N (nautical officer), E (engineer officer), P (polyvalent officer), D (deck crew), R (engine crew), C (catering/saloon rating), G (GP rating), M (medical officer), Y (foreign national) and Z (non-seafarer). DGS has since added H, S and K.
  • The last four digits are a serial number.

You keep the same number when you move up from cadet to Master or from rating to officer. It is printed on your CDC, your STCW course certificates and your CoC. Without an INDoS number you can't join any DGS-approved course, and that includes pre-sea training and basic STCW courses.

Who generates it: the institute, not you

Until 30 April 2019, the INDoS Cell issued numbers. DGS Training Circular No. 08 of 2019 then moved number generation to DGS-approved Maritime Training Institutes (MTIs).

How it works now:

  • You register online and get a reference number.
  • The MTI checks your original documents with you there in person and generates the INDoS number.
  • Profile corrections: DGS has told seafarers to check and update their own profiles on the e-Samudra portal. Some corrections can't be made there, such as name or date of birth. For those, follow the current procedure on the DGS/DGMA website or contact the INDoS Cell (MTT).

DGS's own INDoS page says candidates should not be charged for INDoS generation. If an institute or agent asks for a separate payment just to "get you an INDoS", ask them to put it in writing and check it against the current DGS circulars.

Step-by-step: from registration to INDoS number

Important (2026): DG Shipping moved seafarer services from the old e-Governance portal to the new e-Samudra portal (esamudra.dgshipping.gov.in), which went live on 7 April 2026. The official site is now at dgma.gov.in, so check there for the current name and menus. The steps below follow Circular 08 of 2019, but menu names and screens may now look different. Always follow what the live official website shows.

  1. Get your documents ready (see the next section). Above all, have a valid passport.
  2. Register online on the DGS website as a candidate seeking admission to an MTI. Enter your details exactly as they appear in your passport. Upload a photo or signature if the portal asks for them. The MTI uploads the scanned documents and signatures when it checks your originals.
  3. Note your reference number. After you submit, the system gives you a reference number. It is also sent by email and SMS to the email ID and mobile number you registered.
  4. If you already have an INDoS number, the system shows it on screen instead of creating a new one. Never register again to get a second number.
  5. Join an MTI within three months. The reference number expires three months after it is issued. If it lapses, you have to register again and get a new one.
  6. Go to the MTI in person with your originals and your reference number. The number can only be generated with you physically there.
  7. The MTI checks your documents and applies. The institute checks your originals against what you entered online, then submits the request.
  8. Confirmation. Once the number is generated, you get an email and SMS confirming it.
  9. Check it yourself. Look up your number on the DGS website, in the INDoS / "Master Checker" verification section. DGS treats the Master Checker printout as official proof of your INDoS.

Documents and photo: what the institute will check

Item What DGS asks for
Passport Valid original; registration requires one
Class 10 (10th) pass marksheet Original, unless a DGS circular exempts your category
Photograph Recent passport-size photo with a plain/white background, meeting the specification shown on the portal at registration
Signature As asked for by the portal or the institute
Contact details Your own working email ID and mobile number

Your institute may also ask for papers needed for course admission, such as a medical certificate from a DG-approved doctor. Those are for admission, not for the INDoS number, and they vary by course and institute.

About the photo: DGS uses the same photograph across your records, and employers check the photo and signature in your online profile against you. A selfie, a coloured background or a cropped group photo can cause problems for years.

Common reasons for delay or rejection, and how to avoid them

  • Name or date of birth doesn't match. The data you enter must match your passport exactly, including spelling, order of names and date format. Fixing a mismatch later means going through the correction procedure.
  • Reference number expired. You didn't join an MTI within three months. Register again and get a new reference number.
  • Not there in person, or no originals. Photocopies aren't enough, and the number can't be generated without you there.
  • Photo rejected. The photo doesn't meet the specification shown on the portal, for example because of the wrong background.
  • Duplicate registration. You already have a number from an earlier course or a different category. The system will show the existing number. Use that one, and don't try to get around it.
  • Email or mobile you can't access. Every confirmation, password and later CoC or exam update goes there. Use your own ID, not the agent's or the institute's.
  • Course or number not showing correctly. MTIs upload course and batch data to the DGS system, so raise it with the institute first.

Worked scenario: GP Rating aspirant from Kerala

Arun has passed Class 12 and wants to join a GP Rating course in Chennai.

  • Week 1: He renews his expiring passport first. Registering with a passport that is about to expire just means profile updates later.
  • Week 4: He registers online, enters his name exactly as it appears in the passport, and gets a reference number by email and SMS.
  • Week 6: He reports to the DGS-approved MTI with his original passport and 10th marksheet. The institute checks them and generates his INDoS. The number starts with the year, followed by the category letters for a GP rating.
  • Same week: He checks the number on the DGS website and saves the printout.

What could have gone wrong: If his admission had been delayed past three months, the reference number would have lapsed and he would have had to register again. If a "consultant" had offered him an INDoS without a visit to the institute, that would have been a red flag. The number can't be generated unless the candidate is there with originals.

Corrections, foreign nationals and Filipino seafarers

Correcting your profile: With the move to e-Samudra, DGS has asked every seafarer to log in, check and update their profile on the e-Samudra portal. For corrections you can't make there (e.g. name or date of birth), follow the current procedure on the DGS/DGMA website or contact the INDoS Cell (MTT). Do this before you apply for a CDC, an exam or a CoC, because a wrong profile holds up everything later.

Foreign nationals training at DGS-approved institutes in India can get an INDoS number under the Y category.

Filipino seafarers: INDoS is India's national database. You only need a number if you take DGS-approved training in India. Your own records (SIRB, MARINA certificates) are handled through Philippine authorities.

Oral-exam style: questions an examiner or interviewer may ask

Q: Why does India issue INDoS numbers? A: To keep a central database of Indian seafarers' certificates that can be checked, to stop fraudulent certificates being used, and to meet the STCW requirement to make certificate information available to other administrations and employers.

Q: Does your INDoS number change when you get your CoC? A: No. It is permanent. The category letters show your category when the number was first issued.

Q: How would a company check your INDoS and CoC? A: Through the DGS online verification facility (Master Checker / certificate verification). Employers are expected to check CoCs online.

Q: Under which circular do MTIs generate INDoS numbers? A: DGS Training Circular No. 08 of 2019.

Key points
  • First-timers can't apply directly. Register online, get a reference number, and the DGS-approved MTI generates the INDoS.
  • The reference number is valid for three months. Join an institute within that time or register again.
  • You need your original valid passport and Class 10 marksheet, and you must attend in person.
  • INDoS has eight characters (year, two category letters, four-digit serial), is permanent, and is printed on every certificate.
  • DG Shipping states candidates should not be charged for INDoS generation. Be wary of agents selling it.
  • Seafarer services moved to the e-Samudra portal in 2026, and the official site is now at dgma.gov.in.
  • Check and update your profile on e-Samudra. For corrections you can't make there, follow the current DGS/DGMA procedure or contact the INDoS Cell (MTT).

Frequently asked questions

Can I get an INDoS number without joining a course?

Not as a first-timer. Under Training Circular 08 of 2019, a DGS-approved MTI generates the number when you seek admission there. You register online, then the institute checks your originals and generates the number.

How long is the INDoS reference number valid?

Three months from the date it is issued. If you haven't joined an MTI in that time, register again and a new reference number will be generated.

What documents do I need for an INDoS number?

A valid passport and your original Class 10 pass marksheet, unless a DGS circular exempts you. You also need a recent passport-size photo with a plain/white background that meets the specification shown on the portal at registration. Your institute may want more papers for course admission.

How do I check whether my INDoS number is genuine?

Look it up in the INDoS verification (Master Checker) section of the DG Shipping website. DGS treats the Master Checker printout as official proof. Since 2026 the services are on the e-Samudra portal and the official site is at dgma.gov.in, so follow the current menus there.

My name or date of birth is wrong in my INDoS profile. What do I do?

Log in to the e-Samudra portal and check and update your profile, as DGS has directed. For corrections you can't make there, such as name or date of birth, follow the current procedure on the DGS/DGMA website or contact the INDoS Cell (MTT). Fix it before you apply for a CDC, an exam or a CoC, because every later service uses this profile.

Test yourself

Who generates the INDoS number for a first-time candidate under the current procedure?

Since Training Circular 08 of 2019, MTIs generate INDoS numbers after checking the candidate's originals in person. The INDoS Cell issued numbers until 30 April 2019.

How long is the reference number valid after online registration?

Under DGS Training Circular 08 of 2019, the reference number is valid for three months. After that the candidate must register again.

What happens to your INDoS number when you go from cadet to officer?

DGS states that an INDoS number, once given, doesn't change when the seafarer's category changes (DGS INDoS / MTT FAQ).

Which two original documents are the basic requirements at the MTI for INDoS generation?

Training Circular 08 of 2019 and the DGS INDoS page list a valid passport and the 10th marksheet, unless a DGS circular exempts the candidate.

A candidate who already holds an INDoS number registers again online. What does the system do?

Under Training Circular 08 of 2019, if the candidate already has an INDoS, the system shows the existing number as a message. This prevents duplicate numbers.
